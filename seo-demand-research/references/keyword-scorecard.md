# Keyword Scorecard

Score each keyword from 0-100. Use evidence, not tool metrics alone.

## Weighted Rubric

| Factor | Weight | How to Judge |
|---|---:|---|
| Search demand | 15 | Semrush volume, visible trend, related terms, SERP activity |
| Intent clarity | 15 | User wants a tool/action/result, not vague info |
| Competition reality | 25 | Actual top SERP strength, not just KD |
| Backlink feasibility | 10 | Number and quality of referring domains needed |
| Product fit | 15 | Can a focused MVP satisfy the query well |
| Monetization | 10 | CPC, paid competitors, subscription/export/paywall potential |
| SEO expansion | 10 | Can this keyword support clusters and internal links |

## Competition Reality Rules

Subtract heavily when:

- top 3 are strong SaaS/tools with high traffic;
- top results are old domains with strong topical authority;
- Ahrefs estimates many followed backlinks from many domains;
- SERP has 3+ ads and dominant brands;
- the query is a head term with broad intent.

Add confidence when:

- top results include weak blogs, generic directories, thin tools, Reddit/Quora, or outdated pages;
- top pages answer only part of the intent;
- no competitor offers a good no-signup preview;
- searchers likely need repeated use or export.

## Labels

- `Build now`: score 75+, clear MVP and beatable SERP.
- `Build with support`: score 60-74, needs supporting pages/backlinks.
- `Content support`: useful cluster keyword, not homepage wedge.
- `Watch`: trend/new term, insufficient current demand.
- `Reject`: strong SERP, weak intent, or poor monetization.

## Red Flags

Do not pick a keyword as first wedge if:

- top competitor has hundreds of thousands or millions of visits and directly matches the query;
- Ahrefs says 50+ KD plus many domains/backlinks required;
- the exact query is owned by entrenched education brands;
- the product would be a clone with no meaningful gap;
- the keyword volume is inflated by non-commercial intent.
